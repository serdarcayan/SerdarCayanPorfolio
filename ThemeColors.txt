Portfolio Color Theme
=====================

Theme: Charcoal graphite with muted teal, blue and restrained amber accents.

Core colors
-----------
Color           Hex         Tailwind class                 Usage
Near black      #050505   bg-[#050505]                   Main page background
Graphite        #14181F   bg-[#14181f]                   Glass panels and cards
Primary text    #E8EDF2   text-slate-100                Headings and primary copy
Muted text      #A1AAB5   text-slate-400                Supporting text
Muted teal      #7FC2D4   text-[#7fc2d4]                Logo and primary accent
Blue            #A7BADE   text-blue-300                 Secondary technology accent
Amber           #D9B486   text-[#d9b486]                Restrained highlight and hover accent

Tailwind utility patterns
-------------------------

Accent label:     text-[#91b9bd]
Accent button:    bg-[#426f74] text-white hover:bg-[#54858a]
Warm hover:       hover:text-[#d9b486]
Soft card:        border border-white/10 bg-white/[0.055]
Panel glass:      border border-white/10 bg-[#14181f]/90 backdrop-blur-xl
Muted copy:       text-slate-400

Usage guidance
--------------
- Use near-white for primary headings and readable body copy.
- Use muted teal for interactive elements, section labels and active navigation.
- Reserve amber for small highlights and hover states.
- Keep large surfaces dark and translucent; use the gradient glows sparingly.
- Use slate for secondary copy and supporting labels.
